# Swiggy Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The Swiggy dataset contains **197,430 records** covering **January to August 2025**. Total sales are approximately **₹5.30 Cr**, with an average order value of **₹268.51** and an average rating of **4.34**.

## 1. Sales Performance

- Total Sales: **₹5,30,12,506**

## 8. Recommended Business Actions

1. **Focus on Bengaluru:** Investigate what restaurant mix, pricing, and demand patterns are driving its strong performance.
2. **Leverage weekend demand:** Test Saturday-focused offers, family meals, and combo promotions.
3. **Protect the Veg segment:** Veg contributes about 63.8% of sales, making it a major revenue driver.
4. **Study top restaurant partners:** The top brands contribute a substantial portion of revenue; partnership and promotional strategies can be optimized around them.
5. **Investigate February softness:** Compare restaurant availability, order volume, category mix, and AOV to understand the monthly decline.
6. **Grow secondary markets:** Lucknow, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and New Delhi show strong sales potential and can be evaluated for expansion or targeted campaigns.

## Data Caveat

The insights above are calculated from the fields available in the workbook. `Rating Count` is summed from the provided field and should not automatically be interpreted as a count of unique customers or unique reviews. Q3 is also a partial quarter because the dataset covers January through August 2025.
